# AstraCare AI — Demo Scripts (`DEMO.md`)

> Record in English (or burn in English captions) at 1080p. Check that the uploaded video is **≤ 30.0 s**. Pick an astronaut in a WARNING or CRITICAL state before recording (for example EXP-2 Liam Soren) so the alerts are visible.

## Option A: 30-second video (recommended)

- **[0:00–0:03] Hook** — *Visual:* **Crew** screen with the five astronauts and one critical alert.
  *Caption:* "Eleven minutes from Earth today. A sick astronaut can't wait for a doctor."
- **[0:03–0:07] Name** — *Visual:* sidebar and the astronaut's **Vitals** screen with the 3D anatomical body.
  *Caption:* "AstraCare AI — an onboard medical officer for Challenge 05."
- **[0:07–0:15] Personal baseline + pain map** — *Visual:* tap the chest on the 3D body, report pain, watch the alert and the readings against the personal range.
  *Caption:* "It compares every astronaut with their own baseline — and listens when they say where it hurts."
- **[0:15–0:22] Cause tree + live NASA data** — *Visual:* **Diagnosis** → root cause, then the **Earth link** tab showing the JPL Horizons delay.
  *Caption:* "It checks live NASA space weather before blaming the body, and knows the real light-time to Earth."
- **[0:22–0:27] AI agent** — *Visual:* **Assistant**, ask "Analyze Liam now and alert Earth if it is serious"; show the executed-tools list.
  *Caption:* "An AI agent runs the exam, the diagnosis and the alert to Flight Surgeons — even offline."
- **[0:27–0:30] Close** — *Visual:* Crew screen.
  *Caption:* "AstraCare AI · Built with NASA data · [public URL]"
